Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

EZEE VISION CHAMPUA ("we", "our", "us") operates the EZEE VISION CHAMPUA Student Learning Portal and related application services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our website or mobile application.

We respect your privacy and only use information that is reasonably necessary to provide and improve the learning services offered through EZEE VISION CHAMPUA.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may process the following information:

  • Account information: email address and authentication information required to create and access an account.
  • Profile information: information that may be associated with a student's learning account, such as display name or account-related information.
  • Learning activity: information necessary to provide learning resources, quizzes, notices, favourites, and related portal functionality.
  • Technical information: limited technical information required for the application to function, maintain security, and diagnose technical problems.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only for purposes related to providing the EZEE VISION CHAMPUA learning service, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Authenticating users and maintaining secure sessions.
  • Providing educational materials and learning resources.
  • Providing quizzes, notices, favourites, and related features.
  • Maintaining application security and preventing misuse.
  • Improving reliability and resolving technical issues.

3. Firebase Services

Our application uses Firebase services for authentication and backend data services.

Information processed through Firebase is used to provide account authentication and application functionality.

Firebase may process information according to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

4. Educational Content

Educational resources may be hosted or delivered through third-party content services such as Google Drive. These services are used to provide learning materials to authorized users.

We do not use students' personal information for advertising or sell personal information to third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary for operating the application, such as Firebase and content-hosting services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security and rights of users and the service.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data transmitted between the application and supported services is protected using secure communication mechanisms such as HTTPS where supported.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and related information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain legitimate security records.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely removed in accordance with applicable requirements.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting us using the contact information provided below.

We will review and process valid deletion requests and delete applicable account information, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, security, or legitimate operational purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

EZEE VISION CHAMPUA is an educational learning portal intended for students. We take student privacy seriously.

We do not knowingly use personal information for advertising or sell student personal information.

Where required, use of the service by minors should be undertaken with the involvement and supervision of a parent, guardian, school, or authorized educational institution.
